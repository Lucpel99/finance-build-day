Risk agent tooling

When the algorithm flags a risk,
agents get the full picture.

Instead of hunting through spreadsheets, risk agents see a structured, automated comparison — claimed vs real, transactions vs invoices, all in one view.

Step 1

Claimed vs real

Open banking and open accounting data is automatically compared against what the merchant declared. Every discrepancy is flagged — no manual number-crunching.

Step 2

Invoice matching

Every accounting invoice is matched against real bank transactions. Invoices with no matching payment surface immediately as items that need attention.

Step 3

Side-by-side view

Matched invoice-transaction pairs are aligned row by row. Risk agents immediately see what reconciles and what doesn't — no scrolling between separate screens.
